A LITTLE ABOUT ME

From Muscat
to Vellore.

I’m Ashbel Nixon, a first-year B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student at VIT Vellore. My journey started at Indian School Muscat, Oman, and has continued into a new chapter where I’m learning to turn ideas into useful software. I’m at the beginning of my engineering journey, so I’m focused on building strong fundamentals while putting what I learn into practice.

I’m drawn to both sides of building products: the interface people interact with and the systems that make everything work behind the scenes. I’ve experimented with Python automation and voice commands through Friday, explored an AI-assisted legal information experience with LawLM, and contributed to the frontend of InTheMarket, a second-hand electronics marketplace concept developed with a hackathon team.

Right now, I’m especially curious about AI/ML, backend engineering, full-stack development, and eventually cloud and systems work. I learn best by building, debugging, asking questions, and iterating on real projects. I’m looking to keep improving through collaborations, hackathons, and opportunities to contribute to a team while learning from experienced developers.
